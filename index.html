What is StonkRider?

StonkRider is a viral browser game that turns stock market charts into rideable motocross tracks. Instead of reading charts like a trader, you ride them like a dirt bike racer. Pick any stock or crypto ticker — TSLA, NVDA, BTC-USD, GME — and the price history becomes your terrain.

Created by indie developer Aymeric Dietrich, StonkRider went viral for its clever mashup of finance and arcade gaming. Big price jumps become steep ramps. Market crashes become dangerous drops. Volatile meme stocks create the wildest, most chaotic tracks. The game combines skill-based motorcycle physics with real market data, making every ride unique.

How to Play StonkRider

Controls

Use Arrow Keys or WASD to control your motorcycle. Up/W to accelerate, Down/S to brake, Left/A and Right/D to lean. Space to jump. On mobile, use touch controls.

Objective

Ride as far as possible on the stock chart without crashing. Perform flips for bonus points. The more volatile the stock, the harder and more rewarding the ride. Submit your score to the daily leaderboard.

Pro Tips

Start with stable stocks (AAPL, SPY) to learn the physics. Lean forward on downhills for speed boosts. Time your jumps at the peaks for maximum air and flips. Avoid meme stocks until you've mastered the basics — they're wild.

StonkRider Tips & Tricks

  • Start with less volatile stocks like AAPL or MSFT to learn the controls before tackling TSLA or GME
  • Lean forward (Right Arrow) when going downhill to build speed for big jumps
  • Use the Space bar to jump at the top of peaks — timing is everything for backflips
  • The crash is part of the fun — each crash teaches you the track's tricky sections
  • Check the daily challenge every day — same track for all riders means fair competition
  • Crypto tickers (BTC-USD, ETH-USD) tend to be more volatile and create wilder tracks
